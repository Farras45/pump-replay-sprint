Pump Replay Sprint Bot — an early-launch scalper that trades 10 SOL of paper money on a pump.fun replay. It watches each coin from its CreateEvent, buys once while the coin is young and near its opening price — only after at least three trades from at least three distinct non-creator buyers with little selling against them — and sells the whole position fast on a small take-profit, a tight stop-loss, staleness, a creator sale, or a maximum hold. Fully deterministic: every decision comes from the harness state, with no clock, randomness, environment, or network use.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
